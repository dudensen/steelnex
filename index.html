01 — THE COMPANY

A direct connection
to the steel you need.

Steelnex is a Greece-based steel trading business focused on flat steel coils. We work with international suppliers to serve businesses across Europe, with a particular focus on construction applications.

Every enquiry starts with your requirements. From material specifications to commercial terms, our role is to make the conversation between supplier and customer clear, practical and productive.
