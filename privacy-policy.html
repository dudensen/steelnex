2. Information we process

When you email us, we may receive your name, business email address, company name, product requirements and any information you choose to include. Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs when delivering this website.

3. How the enquiry form works

The form prepares a message in your own email application. It does not send the entered information to a Steelnex website server. Your email application or provider handles the message when you choose to send it. Steelnex receives and processes the enquiry only when the email is sent and delivered.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We use enquiries to respond to requests, discuss products, prepare quotations and manage business relationships. Depending on the circumstances, processing may be necessary for steps toward a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) or for our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and managing commercial communications (Article 6(1)(f)). We may also retain records where required by law (Article 6(1)(c)).

5. Recipients and international transfers

Enquiry emails may be processed by our email and IT service providers, and website requests by our hosting and security providers. We do not sell enquiry details. The providers used, their processing locations, and any transfers outside the European Economic Area should be confirmed against our actual service configurations and relevant safeguards.

6. Retention

We keep enquiries only as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request and related business follow-up, or longer where legal obligations or legal claims require it. The precise retention schedule should be confirmed against Steelnex’s internal email and records policies.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or object to certain processing. Contact info@steelnex.gr to exercise your rights. You may also lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr).

8. Cookies and technical data

The supplied Steelnex website does not itself set analytics or advertising cookies. Infrastructure providers may nevertheless process technical request and security logs. If analytics, third-party embeds or other tracking technologies are added later, this notice and any applicable consent mechanisms must be updated.

9. Updates

We may update this notice when our website, providers or data-processing practices change. The version published here is dated 28 October 2026.
